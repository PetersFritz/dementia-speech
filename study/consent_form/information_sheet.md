## Invitation

You are being invited to take part in a research study. Before you decide,
it's important that you understand why the research is being done and what
it would involve for you. Please take time to read the following
information carefully, and contact the researcher above if anything is
unclear or you would like more information.

## What is the purpose of this study?

We have built a dashboard that presents speech-based measures from two
cognitive assessment tasks (a picture description task and a word-fluency
task) to support clinical decision-making around dementia and mild
cognitive impairment (MCI). This study evaluates how useful and
interpretable the dashboard is when used to make a diagnostic judgement,
and gathers feedback on how it could be improved.

## Do I have to take part?

No. Taking part is entirely voluntary. If you decide to take part, you are
still free to withdraw at any time without giving a reason, and without any
disadvantage. Because your responses are recorded against an anonymous code
rather than your name (see "Confidentiality" below), once you have
completed the study we may not be able to identify and remove your specific
responses from the dataset — you can withdraw at any point *during* the
study with no consequence, but full removal after completion may not be
possible.

## What will happen if I take part?

1. After giving consent (this form), you will automatically receive an
   email with a personal link to the study.
2. Using that link, you will view the dashboard for 20 anonymised cases,
   one at a time. For each case, you will record a diagnostic judgement
   (Healthy Control / MCI / Dementia) and how confident you feel in that
   judgement.
3. After the 20th case, you will be asked to complete a short questionnaire
   about your experience using the dashboard.
4. You can close your browser at any point and return to the same link
   later to continue where you left off.

The whole process is expected to take approximately [X–Y minutes —
PLACEHOLDER, estimate once you've timed a pilot run].

## What data will be collected, and how will it be used?

- **Your email address** is collected only so we can send you your personal
  study link, and so we can re-send it if you lose it. It is stored in a
  Google Sheet accessible only to the research team, separately from your
  responses.
- **Your responses within the study** (diagnostic judgements, confidence
  ratings, questionnaire answers) are recorded against an anonymous code
  generated for you — the study system itself never stores your email
  address alongside your responses.
- The anonymous code is, however, linked to your email in the Google Sheet
  described above (this is what lets you resume the study from the same
  link). Your data is therefore **pseudonymised, not fully anonymous** — the
  research team could in principle re-identify your responses via that
  link, but will not do so except as required (e.g. if you ask to withdraw
  your data). [Adjust this paragraph if you plan to delete the email/token
  linkage after the recruitment window closes — if so, say so here and it
  becomes closer to full anonymisation after that point.]
- All data is stored on University of Sheffield-managed infrastructure (not
  third-party cloud services).
- The 20 cases you assess are themselves de-identified data from an
  existing, separately consented research dataset — you will not be shown
  any information that could identify the individuals whose speech is
  represented.

## Will my participation be confidential?

Yes, as described above — your name is never attached to your diagnostic
judgements or questionnaire answers within the study itself.
