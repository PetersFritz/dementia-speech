# Participant Information Sheet

## Co-design and feedback sessions

**Project title:** [Project title]

You are being invited to take part in a research project. Before you decide, it is important for you to understand why the research is being done and what it will involve. Please take time to read the following information carefully, and feel free to ask us anything that is not clear or that you would like more information about.

### What is the project about?

This project is developing a clinician-facing "decision support dashboard" that presents speech-based features from established cognitive assessment tasks (verbal fluency and the "Cookie Theft" picture description) in an interpretable visual form, to support clinical reasoning about whether a case reflects healthy cognition, mild cognitive impairment, or dementia. As part of developing the dashboard, we are seeking input from clinicians and researchers to shape its design and to give feedback on a full draft before it is used in a later evaluation study.

### Why have I been invited?

You have been invited as a member of the CognoSpeak group because of your expertise in the underlying technology and/or in clinical assessment. Your input will help us make the dashboard clear, useful, and appropriate for clinical reasoning.

### Do I have to take part?

No. Taking part is entirely voluntary. It is up to you to decide whether to take part, and you may withdraw at any time without giving a reason. Because you are a colleague within the CognoSpeak group, we want to be clear that whether or not you take part, and any decision to withdraw, will have no effect whatsoever on your role, your working relationships, or your standing within the group or the University. There is no obligation or expectation to take part.

### What will happen if I take part?

You are being invited to contribute at one or both of two stages:

- A **co-design focus group** early in development, where you will be shown an early version of the dashboard and asked open questions about its content, layout, clarity, and usefulness. This will run as one of two sessions of approximately 60 minutes each, and you can attend whichever session suits your schedule.
- A **feedback session** later on, where you will be shown a full draft of the dashboard and asked for structured feedback before it is finalised.

Each session is held as a hybrid meeting, so you may attend either in person or online via Google Meet, whichever is more convenient.

### Will the session be recorded?

With your consent, the session will be recorded so that we can transcribe what was discussed. Because Google Meet records audio and video together, we will extract the audio and produce a transcript, and then delete the video recording. Only an anonymised transcript is kept for analysis. The audio is transcribed using Whisper, an open-source speech-recognition tool, run locally on University-managed computing infrastructure, so the recording is not sent to any external transcription service.

### How will what I say be used?

The transcripts will be analysed to identify themes that inform the dashboard's design. This analysis is part of the research: the findings will be written up and published in academic outputs (such as journal articles, conference papers, and theses). Your data will only ever appear in these outputs in anonymised or aggregated form, and you will not be identifiable. If we use any direct quotations, they will be anonymised; because the group is small, we will take particular care that individuals cannot be identified from quoted or attributed material. You will be asked separately on the consent form whether you agree to the use of anonymised quotations.

Your anonymised data may also be shared with other researchers in the CognoSpeak group for research purposes connected to this project. They will use it only for those research purposes. Your data will not be shared with anyone outside the project, and no identifiable data is shared in this way.

### What are the possible disadvantages or risks of taking part?

We do not anticipate any significant risks. The main cost to you is the time involved (approximately 60 minutes per session). You are free to decline to answer anything, or to stop, at any point.

### What are the possible benefits of taking part?

There is no direct personal benefit, but your input will help shape a tool intended to support clinical assessment of cognitive decline.

### How will my information be kept confidential, and what will happen to my data?

Your personal data (for example the recording and transcript, and your contact details) will be stored securely on University-managed systems and accessed only by the immediate research team. Identifiable data will be kept only as long as necessary and destroyed within six months of the end of the project (expected 17 September 2027, giving a destruction date of 17 March 2028). Anonymised transcripts, which no longer identify you, may be retained for analysis and publication.

The University of Sheffield is the Data Controller for this project. The University processes personal data for research in the public interest.
