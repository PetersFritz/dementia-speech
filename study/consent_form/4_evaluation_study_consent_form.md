> **Note:** Draft content for the online consent form (Phase 3), presented as a Google Form together with the information sheet. This is separate from, and completed before, the study application itself: submitting this form triggers an automated email containing the participant's personal study link, a copy of the information sheet, and a record of their consent responses. Each statement is an affirmative tick the participant must select; the form cannot be submitted until all required statements are ticked. Text in **[square brackets]** is a placeholder to complete.
